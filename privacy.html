SWAWM privacy

SWAWM (“So When Are We Meeting?”) is a scheduling page. This describes what the app stores when you use it.

What we collect

  • Event titles, dates, and hours you create, stored in Google Firebase (project Meeting Sync).
  • The times you mark as free, under the name you type.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, name, and email.
  • If you use Fill My Availability: whether you are busy or free on your Google Calendars. Event titles and details are not read and are not stored.

How it is used

That data is only used to show group availability and to fill Google Calendar invite links. Emails of signed-in people may appear to others who have the event link, so they can be added as calendar guests.

Who can see it

Anyone with an event link can read that event and its responses. We do not sell this data.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
